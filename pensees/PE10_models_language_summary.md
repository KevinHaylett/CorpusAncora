## Core Claim

A model is always a model. Its success does not make it identical with the world. Every model carries a double boundary — **exogenous** (the instruments available to produce registrations) and **endogenous** (the symbolic and linguistic capacities available to represent, name, and reason) — and any scientific epoch is defined by both simultaneously. When either boundary shifts, a new epoch becomes possible.

The central formulation:

> **Epoch E = {available exogenous distinctions, available endogenous distinctions}**  
> **New epoch ≈ improved exogenous measurement + revised endogenous measurement**

The paper argues that six distinct types of prediction must be carefully distinguished; that residual terms in equations carry a hidden cost called **symbolic drag**; that the apparent noun/verb distinction is a function of measurement resolution; that the large language model is a new kind of **endogenous instrument** available to any researcher; and that Kuhn's passive model of anomaly accumulation can now be replaced by an **active programme of deliberately engineering epochal transitions**.

PE10 is a two-chapter Pensée with two appendices. ## Chapter 1 — Models, Measurement, and the Language That Holds Them

### Section 1 — Orientation: A Model Remains a Model

The opening observation is both elementary and often forgotten: a model's success hides the conditions of its formation. A useful equation does not stop being a finite symbolic object. The paper asks: what measurements, symbols, bases, histories, and admissibility rules make its working possible? This question is not philosophical decoration — it locates where models can fail, be extended, or be replaced.

---

### Section 2 — Scientific Epochs and the Double Boundary

Scientific epochs are bounded by two kinds of limit that are usually treated separately but must be understood together:

**Exogenous boundary** — the instruments, detectors, and registering technologies available to produce measurements. Galileo's telescope, Hooke's microscope, Pascal's barometry, Huygens's pendulum clock, the interferometer, the CCD — each shifts the exogenous boundary.

**Endogenous boundary** — the symbolic, mathematical, and linguistic capacities available to represent, reason about, and communicate what has been measured. Euclidean geometry, calculus, probability theory, tensor analysis, information theory, NDS tools, large language models — each shifts the endogenous boundary.

The formal statement:

> **Epoch E = {available exogenous distinctions, available endogenous distinctions}**

A new epoch requires *both* boundaries to move. Improved instrumentation without symbolic tools to represent its output does not produce a new epoch — it produces anomalies. Improved symbolic tools without new exogenous measurement produce formal extensions that float free of registration. The coupling is what matters: **world interaction → registration → symbol → model → instrument → new interaction**.

---

### Section 3 — Exogenous and Endogenous Measurement

**Exogenous measurement** (§3.1): detector registrations, spectra, images, contact readings, sensor outputs. Whatever the world produces when instruments are directed at it. Carries provenance, instrument calibration, uncertainty, resolution limits.

**Endogenous measurement** (§3.2): definition, proof, derivation, simulation, semantic analysis, classification. The symbolic work of producing structure from what has been registered. Includes: choosing a coordinate system, defining a unit, constructing a proof, running a model, naming a residual, categorising a result.

**Their coupling** (§3.3): exogenous and endogenous measurement are not sequential — they are iteratively coupled. The instrument is extended by what the model produces; the model is revised by what the instrument registers. A residual in the equation is an endogenous record of a gap in the exogenous-endogenous loop.

---

### Section 4 — What Prediction Means

The paper insists that *prediction* is not a single thing. Six distinct types are defined and must be distinguished before any claim is made or evaluated:

1. **Retrodictive accommodation** — fitting the model to data already in hand. Numerically impressive but provides no genuine test. (Example: fitting galaxy rotation curves with dark matter halos post-hoc.)

2. **Model-internal consequence** — deriving a result that follows necessarily from the model's structure but was not explicitly put in. A genuine epistemic gain even without new data. (Example: hydrogen stability emerging from the measurement-first inversion of classical mechanics.)

3. **Algebraic compatibility or reinterpretation** — showing that a known result can be rearranged or re-derived within a new framework. Demonstrates structural reach without claiming novel empirical content. (Example: fine-structure constant rearrangement meα³ ≈ e²fRμ₀^(3/2)ε₀^(1/2) — algebraic compatibility, not an independent measurement.)

4. **Structural out-of-sample prediction** — applying the model to cases that were not in the fitting set. A stronger claim than retrodiction. (Example: applying the k-inversion from Mercury/elliptical orbits to other planets and quasars.)

5. **Prospective quantitative prediction** — a numerical prediction made before the measurement is taken, with a specified uncertainty. The classical gold standard of scientific prediction.

6. **Trajectory prediction** (FSM addition) — stabilising onto a related finite symbolic trajectory and predicting the *qualitative structure* of the continuation. A new type made visible by the Finite Symbolic Mechanics framework: what attractor does the current trajectory lead toward?

A **claim classification ledger** is introduced (see Appendix B) in which every result in the Finite Mechanics programme is assigned its prediction type before any comparison or evaluation is made.

### Section 5 — Re-reading the Earlier Finite Mechanics Programme

Five results from the earlier FSM programme are re-examined through the claim classification framework:

**5.1 Galaxy rotation curves** — the observed velocity profile v²_obs is decomposed as v²_obs = v²_luminous + v²_implicit. The implicit mass shell M_implicit,shell(r) = (v²_obs − v²_luminous)r/G is recovered as a model-internal consequence, not an independent entity. Classification: retrodictive accommodation (the fit) + model-internal consequence (the implicit function itself).

**5.2 Mercury / other planets / quasars** — the perihelion shift and quasar deflection are read as evidence for k-inversion. Classification: structural out-of-sample prediction when applied beyond the initial calibration case.

**5.3 Inversion of k and k'** — the core algebraic move: m_implicit = ka, a_implicit = k'm, where k' = 1/k. The mass-acceleration asymmetry is a consequence of the inversion structure, not an empirical input. Classification: model-internal consequence.

**5.4 Hydrogen stability** — the stability of the hydrogen atom under the measurement-first reinterpretation emerges without requiring quantum mechanical postulates to suppress the classical radiation problem. Classification: model-internal consequence.

**5.5 Alpha and the network of constants** — the rearrangement meα³ ≈ e²fRμ₀^(3/2)ε₀^(1/2) shows that the fine-structure constant can be positioned within a network of measured relationships. Classification: algebraic compatibility. It shows structural reach; it is not an independent derivation of α from more fundamental quantities.

### Section 6 — Residual Terms, Implicit Functions, and Symbolic Drag

**Symbolic drag** is introduced as the finite cost of compressing a thicker measurement trajectory into a simpler equation. When an equation works but leaves a residual, that residual is not noise — it is the symbolic record of the gap between what the measurement trajectory carried and what the equation retained.

Implicit functions (dark matter halos, cosmological constant, epicycles) are a specific form of symbolic drag: they absorb the gap between model and measurement by naming the gap rather than resolving it. This is not wrong — it is often necessary and productive. But it must be recognised as what it is.

The Geofinite account: every equation is a finite symbolic artefact held in a measurement loop. The loop includes the instrument, the registration, the symbol, the model, and the return to the world. Symbolic drag accumulates when the loop is not closed — when the equation is detached from the measurement conditions that produced it and treated as self-standing.

### Section 7 — Finite Representation and the Failure of Simple Base Invariance

A number written in base 10 and the same number written in base 2 are *abstractly* the same value. But as finite symbolic objects they are not identical: the sequence of symbols differs, the intermediate results of computation differ, the rounding behaviour differs, and the cognitive accessibility of structure differs.

**Finite base dependence**: abstract value invariant ≠ finite representation invariant.

The implications:
- Equations carry their base as a hidden parameter.
- Compression, approximation, and truncation all interact with the base chosen.
- The appearance of simplicity in a formula is partly a base-dependent property.
- Cross-base translation is an endogenous measurement operation with its own error profile.

This connects directly to the endogenous boundary of an epoch: the symbolic tools available — including the notational systems — constrain what can be expressed, computed, and compared.

### Section 8 — The Noun as a Slow Verb or Slow Noun

The noun/verb distinction is not a fact about the world — it is a function of the measurement window relative to the rate of change of what is being measured.

**Formal statement:** apparent category = f(rate of change, measurement window, resolution)

A **slow verb** is a process so slow relative to the measurement window that it appears as a stable object. A **slow noun** is a stable descriptor that carries — when examined at higher resolution or over longer observation — a residual dynamic structure.

Examples: a mountain is a slow verb (geological uplift); a species is a slow verb (evolutionary drift); a word is a slow noun (semantic trajectory, not fixed meaning); an equation is a slow noun (held in a measurement loop that continues to evolve even as the equation remains formally unchanged).

The Generon is the Geofinite instrument for stabilising slow verbs into slow nouns with explicit provenance: a deliberately stabilised category that carries visible memory of the measurement conditions that produced the stability.

### Section 9 — How Language Holds an Equation

An equation is not a timeless object. It is a finite symbolic artefact produced at a specific point in a measurement trajectory, held in language, transmitted between practitioners, and returned to the world in specific experimental and computational contexts.

Language holds the equation by: naming its variables (endogenous measurement), specifying its units (provenance), constraining its domain of application (admissibility), recording its derivation history (trajectory), and enabling its transmission and critique (communication channel).

When language is treated as a neutral transmission medium — merely carrying the equation from one place to another — this holding function becomes invisible. The measurement conditions encoded in the naming choices, unit conventions, and domain restrictions are treated as incidental rather than load-bearing.

Geofinitism treats language as a functional symbolic trajectory: not neutral transmission but active participation in the measurement loop.

### Section 10 — Model Pluralism Without Arbitrariness

Different models applied to the same phenomenon are not equally arbitrary. They are accountable through:
- The distinctions they preserve (what the measurement trajectory retains)
- The measurements they predict (quantitative and structural)
- The uncertainty they carry (what they claim and what they concede)

Model pluralism is appropriate because different measurement windows, instruments, and symbolic frameworks legitimately emphasise different aspects of the phenomenon. It does not follow that all models are equivalent. The differences are traceable — not to authority or convention — but to the specific exogenous and endogenous resources each model engages.

### Section 11 — The LLM as a New Endogenous Instrument

The large language model is positioned as a new kind of endogenous instrument: it operates within language, traverses symbolic relations at scale, and makes errors that reveal inherited commitments.

Key properties of the LLM as endogenous instrument:
- It traverses symbolic relations that no human practitioner could survey unaided.
- Its outputs are locally coherent within the current symbolic basin of the training corpus.
- Its errors are diagnostic: they reveal where the symbolic trajectory is thin, inherited uncritically, or locked inside an epoch's endogenous boundary.
- It enables a practitioner to probe the endogenous landscape of a discipline — to map the attractors in the symbolic basin — more systematically than was previously possible.

The LLM does not extend the *exogenous* boundary. It does not produce new registrations from the world. It extends the *endogenous* boundary: what can be expressed, navigated, combined, and critiqued symbolically.

A new form of incommensurability arises: LLM outputs are locally coherent within current symbolic conventions yet resist clean translation into stabilised Geofinite categories. This is a diagnostic rather than a defect — it marks the boundary of the current endogenous epoch.

### Section 12 — A Coupled Method of Research and Correction

The research method that emerges from the foregoing is iterative and trajectory-based:

1. Construct an initial model with explicit provenance (which measurements, which symbolic framework, which base, which admissibility rules).
2. Classify every claim before presenting it (using the six prediction types).
3. Apply the LLM as an endogenous probe — not to generate conclusions but to traverse the symbolic neighbourhood of the model and reveal what is coherently connected and what is isolated.
4. Return to the measurement trajectory: where the LLM reveals incoherence, look for the gap in the exogenous-endogenous loop.
5. Apply an explicit human halting condition: the researcher decides when the correction trajectory has converged sufficiently.

The LLM is a tool within a measurement loop, not a replacement for it.

---

### Section 13 — A Practical Research Protocol

A twelve-step protocol is specified:

1. State the model with full provenance: what measurements, instruments, symbolic framework, base, and admissibility rules constitute it.
2. Separate construction data from test data explicitly — do not mix the data used to build the model with the data used to evaluate it.
3. Classify every claim by type (using the six-type taxonomy) before reporting any result.
4. Freeze the prediction rule before the measurement is taken for prospective quantitative predictions.
5. Carry dimensions, uncertainty, and units throughout. Do not drop them at intermediate stages.
6. Compare models symmetrically: apply the same claim-type classification to the competing model as to the proposed one.
7. Identify measurable discriminators: what observation would distinguish the models? If none can be specified, the comparison is symbolic, not empirical.
8. Record every residual without immediately naming it. Preserve the gap before filling it.
9. Examine base, alphabet, and finite precision as explicit parameters. Where are truncation, rounding, or base-dependent artefacts operative?
10. Use the LLM as an endogenous probe: ask it to traverse the symbolic neighbourhood of the model and return what is coherently connected.
11. Archive the full trajectory: not only the result but the sequence of adjustments, corrections, and revisions that produced it.
12. Apply an explicit halting condition: state the criterion by which the current correction cycle will be judged sufficient.

## Chapter 2 — Anomalies, Incommensurability, and the Active Use of New Endogenous Instruments

### Kuhn's Model Revisited

Kuhn's account of scientific change is engaged directly. In the standard Kuhnian picture, anomalies accumulate passively within a normal science phase: practitioners work within a paradigm, anomalies are noticed and set aside, and when they accumulate sufficiently a crisis erupts and a new paradigm emerges. The transition is fundamentally discontinuous and largely beyond the practitioner's control.

The paper accepts the broad structure but challenges the passivity of anomaly accumulation. Kuhn wrote before NDS tools, compression theory, or large-scale symbolic traversal were available. His model is itself a finite symbolic construction shaped by the endogenous capacities available in 1962. It should be treated as such — as an epoch-bound proposal, not an eternal description of how science works.

### Active Incommensurability Search

The key shift: instead of waiting for anomalies to accumulate, practitioners equipped with new endogenous instruments can actively search for incommensurability.

**Incommensurability** (in the Kuhnian sense) — the failure of full translation between paradigms — is not merely a post-hoc description of what happened during a revolution. It is a symptom of the endogenous boundary between epochs. Where two symbolic frameworks fail to translate cleanly, the boundary between epochs is visible.

The LLM makes this searchable: ask it to translate a result from one symbolic framework into another; the places where coherent translation breaks down are candidate incommensurability points. These are not random or arbitrary — they are structurally informative.

### LLMs as a New Kind of Anomaly

The large language model produces a distinctive form of anomaly: outputs that are locally coherent within the current endogenous basin yet resist translation into stabilised Geofinite categories. This is not a failure of the LLM — it is evidence of the endogenous boundary.

A new endogenous instrument, like a new exogenous instrument, produces registrations that the existing symbolic framework cannot fully absorb. The telescope produced observations that Aristotelian celestial mechanics could not accommodate. The LLM produces symbolic traversals that current epistemology of science cannot fully classify. Both are boundary events — markers of where the current epoch's endogenous tools are reaching their limit.

### Engineered Epochal Transitions

The possibility that emerges: instead of waiting for a paradigm to collapse under accumulated anomaly, a research programme can actively work toward an **engineered epochal transition** — deliberately identifying the incommensurability points, targeting the exogenous measurements that would discriminate between frameworks, and applying new endogenous instruments to traverse the symbolic distance between them.

This is not guaranteed to succeed. Epochal transitions remain difficult and partly unpredictable. But they become more legible and more feasible when:
- The double boundary (exogenous + endogenous) is explicitly tracked.
- Claims are classified by type before comparison.
- New endogenous instruments are applied as diagnostic tools rather than oracles.
- Incommensurability is searched for actively rather than awaited passively.

The reversal from passive recipient of anomaly to **active inquirer into incommensurability** is the chapter's central contribution.

## Appendix A — Working Definitions

Thirteen terms are formally defined:

- **Model** — a finite symbolic structure that produces predictions from inputs, constructed from specific measurements, bases, and admissibility rules.
- **Exogenous measurement** — registrations produced by instruments interacting with the world.
- **Endogenous measurement** — symbolic operations (definition, proof, simulation, semantic analysis) producing structure from registrations.
- **Epoch** — a structured period defined by its available exogenous and endogenous distinctions.
- **Retrodiction** — fitting a model to data already in hand.
- **Structural prediction** — applying a model to cases not used in its construction.
- **Trajectory prediction** — predicting the qualitative structure of the continuation of a finite symbolic trajectory.
- **Compatibility result** — showing that a result can be rearranged or re-derived within a new framework.
- **Symbolic drag** — the residual cost of compressing a measurement trajectory into a simpler symbolic form.
- **Slow verb / slow noun** — a process or descriptor whose category is a function of measurement window and rate of change.
- **Finite base dependence** — the property that abstract value invariance does not entail finite representation invariance.
- **Endogenous instrument** — a symbolic tool that extends the endogenous boundary of an epoch (examples: calculus, probability theory, LLMs).
- **Halting condition** — the researcher-applied criterion by which an iterative correction trajectory is judged sufficiently converged.
